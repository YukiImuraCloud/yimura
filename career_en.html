Research Interests

My primary research interest is clouds. Clouds interact with small particles (aerosols), precipitation, and radiation, thereby influencing the Earth's climate. These processes are highly complex and represent one of the largest sources of uncertainty in projections of global warming.

I investigate how clouds evolve and how they affect the Earth's climate by studying microphysical processes occurring within clouds.

To better understand cloud microphysical processes, I use a variety of tools, including satellite observations, climate models, and satellite simulators that simulate satellite observations within models and enable consistent comparisons between model output and satellite data.

Currently, I am working with observational data from EarthCARE (Earth Cloud Aerosol and Radiation Explorer), a joint mission between Japan and Europe, focusing on the validation of satellite observations and the integration of EarthCARE observations with climate models.
